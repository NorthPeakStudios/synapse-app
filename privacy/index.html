Privacy Policy

Synapse · last updated October 19, 2026

Short version: no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your cards live on your device. An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email, an encrypted (hashed) password and your study progress so it can sync. Nothing is sold, and you can delete your account in Settings at any time.

Who we are

Synapse is a study app for flashcards, courses and practice tools in medicine, pharmacology, neuroscience and medicinal chemistry, made by Northpeak Studios, an independent student developer. It is for learning only and is not medical advice.

What stays on your device

Your decks, cards, reviews, course progress, streak, XP, settings, voice notes and photos you add to cards are saved on your device. They aren’t sent anywhere unless you sign in to an account or turn on one of the features below.

Apart from the optional account (email) and the optional website purchase form (phone number), we never ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts. There are no advertising IDs, trackers or analytics tools in the app.

Microphone and photos

The microphone is only used when you tap a hands-free review or voice-note feature. Speech recognition is done by your phone’s own speech service. Recordings and photos you add stay on your device unless you add them to a deck you share or sync.

Your password and sync code

Account passwords are stored only as a salted, one-way hash, so nobody (including us) can read them. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited. If you forget your password, email northpeak.support@gmail.com from your account’s email and we’ll send you a one-time reset code.

A sync code works like a password. Anyone who has it can open your synced cards, so keep it private. We can’t recover a lost code.

Deleting your data

  • To delete your account, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. It deletes your email, password hash, synced progress, share links and reminder settings from our server right away.
  • In the app, go to Settings → Data & Sync → Delete my data. It erases everything on the device and, if you choose, deletes the synced copy, your share links and reminder settings from our server right away.
  • Uninstalling the app or removing it from your Home Screen erases the data on that device.
  • Turning off a share link stops it working right away.

Sharing and selling

We don’t sell or rent any data and don’t share it with advertisers. Stored data is kept by our hosting provider only to run the features above. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).

Children and teens

The app is meant for teens and adults and isn’t directed at children under 13. The only personal information it can ask for is an email for the optional account and a phone number for the optional website purchase.
