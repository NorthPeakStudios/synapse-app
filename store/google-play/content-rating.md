# Synapse: App content answers (Play Console -> Policy -> App content)

## Content rating (IARC questionnaire)
- Category: **Reference, News, or Educational**
- Violence, blood, gore: **No.** The Lab shows real medical images (skin conditions, scans, histology slides, surgical instruments) for study, but no violence and no gore. If a question asks about realistic medical or anatomical imagery, answer it honestly with "educational medical images".
- Sexual content or nudity: **No.** (No reproductive anatomy is in the app.)
- Language / profanity: **No**
- Controlled substances: **No** promotion or use. Pharmacology lessons teach how drug classes work for study. There are no dosing amounts and nothing recreational.
- Gambling or simulated gambling: **No**
- User-generated content shared with other users: **No public sharing.** Optional deck share links are private links the user sends to someone themselves. There is no chat, no profiles and no public feed. If the questionnaire asks "Can users interact or exchange content?", answer **No** for public interaction. If Google treats share links as content exchange, answer Yes and note that links are private and revocable in Settings.
- Location sharing: **No**
- **In-app purchases: Yes** (digital goods: Synapse Pro, a one-time $35 lifetime unlock)
- Unrestricted internet access / web browser: **No**

Expected result: Everyone / PEGI 3 to Teen / PEGI 12. Accept whatever IARC returns.

## Target audience and content
- Target age groups: **13-15, 16-17, 18 and over**. Do NOT select under 13 (that would put the app under the Families policy).
- Appeals to children: **No**

## Ads
- Contains ads: **No**

## In-app purchases (store listing shows "In-app purchases")
- Yes, through Google Play Billing only: one product, synapse_pro_lifetime ($35, one-time, non-consumable). No subscriptions.

## Health apps declaration
- Select **Medical reference and education** (or the closest "education" option). Synapse is not a medical device, does not diagnose, and has an in-app "For learning only, not medical advice" note (Lab footer and About).

## Government app / financial features / news app
- All **No**.
