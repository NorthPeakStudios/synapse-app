Full description (max 4000, this is 2165):
Synapse is a study app for curious students who want to really learn medicine and the science behind it, not just cram it.

SPACED-REPETITION FLASHCARDS
Make your own decks and Synapse schedules every review for the moment you are about to forget it. Weak Spots collects the cards you keep missing, Stats shows your progress, and a daily goal keeps your streak going.

200 GUIDED COURSES
Four subject areas: medicine, pharmacology, medicinal chemistry and neurology. Every lesson is written at five levels, from "explain it like I'm 5" up to doctor level, and ends with a short test. Missed questions turn into flashcards with one tap.

A HANDS-ON LAB
Learn by doing with interactive tools: a patient simulator, an ECG rhythm trainer, a CT and MRI slice reader, heart and lung sounds, a lesion locator, lab panel and vitals interpreters, a 3D anatomy viewer, a drug class matcher, an interactions game and more.

DOCTORDLE
A new diagnosis puzzle every day. Read the clues, guess the condition in six tries, then learn how it works.

MADE FOR STUDYING ANYWHERE
- Works offline. Courses, decks and tools are built into the app.
- Optional sync with a private code, so your cards and progress follow you to your other devices. No account, no email, no tracking, no ads.
- Study reminders at the time you choose.
- Export or delete all of your data from Settings at any time.

SYNAPSE PRO
Synapse is free to download and use: your own flashcards, studying, stats, the daily challenge, the daily Doctordle, the first level of every course and a selection of Lab tools. Synapse Pro unlocks every course level and test, the full Lab, .apkg deck import, image occlusion, unlimited Doctordle, themes and app icons.
- Synapse Pro is $35, one time. Yours forever: no subscription, nothing renews.
- Payment is charged to your Google Play account.

FOR LEARNING ONLY
Synapse is an educational tool. It is not medical advice and must not be used to diagnose or treat anyone. Always ask a qualified professional about health questions.
